# 💎 1. Marble Count
*   **id**: MATH-001
*   **domain**: Math/quantitative
*   **difficulty**: Medium (Starting Level)
*   **concept**: Conditional Probability, Combination Summation
*   **puzzle_text**: A jar contains a mix of red and blue marbles. If you randomly pull out exactly two marbles, the probability of them being the same color is 0.75. What is the ratio of red to blue marbles in the jar?
*   **solution_details**:
    *   *Answer:* The ratio of red to blue marbles is 1:2 or 2:1.
    *   *Reasoning Path:* The problem requires summing two independent probabilistic events: P(Red and Red) + P(Blue and Blue) = 0.75. By generalizing the formula and solving for R and B, the ratio must be determined.
    *   *Load-Bearing Concept:* The failure to account for the *sum* of all mutually exclusive paths (i.e., recognizing that P(same color) = P(RR) + P(BB)).
